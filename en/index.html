THE PROBLEM

AI spreads through your organization
faster than the controls that govern it.

Most organizations only realize AI risk when it has already happened. Shadow AI, undocumented models and missing access controls create an annual exposure of €5-50M in risk.

You don't know how many AI tools you use

Employees use Copilot, ChatGPT, Grammarly — most of it without registration or approval.

You don't know who owns them

EU AI Act requires a named owner for every high-risk AI system. No name — instant fine risk.

You don't see them drift

Silent model drift, bias accumulation, scope creep — building up unnoticed over months.

No measurable ROI

AI cost is unplannable, value-return is accidental. The Board can't answer: is it worth it?
